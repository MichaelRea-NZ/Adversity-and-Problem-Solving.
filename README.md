A repostory of materials from my "Does Adversity Create Better Problem Solvers ?" presentation at Code Camp Christchurch Saturday 21st November 2026.
Presently there is a worksheet "Personal_Problem_Solving_Challenge.pdf" that people can use as a practical exercise designed to help individuals develop 
their problem-solving approach when faced with difficult or challenging situations.
It encourages people to step back, examine how they respond to problems, identify what they can influence and consider practical ways forward.
The exercise consists of five questions that guide the user through a process of recognising, analysing and responding to adversity.
More materials will be added after 21st November.
